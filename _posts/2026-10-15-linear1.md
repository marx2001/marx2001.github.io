# 线性代数的拆分思想

## 一、矩阵乘向量

已知：

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}
\]
</div>

**拆分矩阵：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M=\left(
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf m_1},\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf m_2}
\right)
\]
</div>

**线性组合：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\begin{aligned}
M\mathbf v
&=1\mathbf m_1+1\mathbf m_2\\
&=1\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
 +1\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}}
\end{aligned}
\]
</div>

**结论：矩阵乘向量 = 列向量的线性组合。**

---

## 二、矩阵乘矩阵

已知：

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
M_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
</div>

**拆分右侧矩阵：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M_1=\left(
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf v_1},\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-2\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf v_2}
\right)
\]
</div>

**分别变换：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_2\mathbf v_1
&=1\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
 +1\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}\\[10pt]
M_2\mathbf v_2
&=-2\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
 +0\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}0\\-2\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
\]
</div>

**重新组合：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_2M_1
&=\left(M_2\mathbf v_1,\,M_2\mathbf v_2\right)\\
&=\left(
\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix},\,
\begin{pmatrix}0\\-2\end{pmatrix}
\right)\\
&=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}}
\end{aligned}
\]
</div>

**结论：右侧矩阵拆分为列向量 → 逐列变换 → 重新组合。**

---

## 三、矩阵的降维变换

已知：

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
M_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\1&0\\3&4\end{pmatrix}
\]
</div>

**拆分右侧矩阵：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M_1=\left(
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\3\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf v_1},\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-2\\0\\4\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf v_2}
\right)
\]
</div>

**拆分左侧矩阵：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
M_2=\left(
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf m_1},\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf m_2},\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf m_3}
\right)
\]
</div>

**分别变换：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_2\mathbf v_1
&=1\mathbf m_1+1\mathbf m_2+3\mathbf m_3\\
&=1\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}
 +1\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
 +3\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}\\[10pt]
M_2\mathbf v_2
&=-2\mathbf m_1+0\mathbf m_2+4\mathbf m_3\\
&=-2\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}
 +0\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
 +4\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\0\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
\]
</div>

**重新组合：**

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\begin{aligned}
M_2M_1
&=\left(M_2\mathbf v_1,\,M_2\mathbf v_2\right)\\
&=\left(
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\,
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\0\end{pmatrix}
\right)\\
&=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\1&0\end{pmatrix}}
\end{aligned}
\]
</div>

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
(2\times3)(3\times2)=(2\times2),\qquad
\mathbb R^3\xrightarrow{\ M_2\ }\mathbb R^2
\]
</div>

**结论：每个三维列向量经过变换，得到二维列向量。**

---

## 四、总结

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\boxed{A\mathbf v=\sum_{i=1}^{n}v_i\mathbf a_i}
\]
</div>

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\boxed{AB=\left(A\mathbf b_1,\,A\mathbf b_2,\,\ldots,\,A\mathbf b_p\right)}
\]
</div>

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
\boxed{A_{m\times n}B_{n\times p}=C_{m\times p}}
\]
</div>

矩阵乘法一般**不满足交换律**，但**满足结合律**：

<div class="math-display" markdown="0">
\[
AB\ne BA\quad(\text{一般情形}),\qquad (AB)C=A(BC)
\]
</div>

**核心：拆分列向量 → 分别线性变换 → 重新组合。**
